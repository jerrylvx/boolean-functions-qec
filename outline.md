# Virginia Tech Applied Algebra Seminar, October 22, 2026

Target: 40 minutes plus discussion within the one-hour slot. Audience: coding theorists, mathematicians, and QEC researchers. Four opening slides cover quantum error correction, CSS codes as pairs of binary codes, the limits on transversal gates, and three codes with different transversal gates. The [[8,3,2]] cube code then introduces the notation over seven slides, and the 48-point support of Q48 is the common picture for the construction. Overlay steps build on these two objects. Full references appear at the bottom of the first slide that cites them.
