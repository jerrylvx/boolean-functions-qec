## Nezami–Haah source checks

Definition 1 quantifies over all triples in a subspace, including repeated vectors. A unital space contains the all-ones vector. Odd logical rows of a Bravyi–Haah matrix therefore do not make its full row span a triorthogonal space. Puncturing/coordinate deletion of a parent unital space produces descendant codes. For distinct generator columns and an all-one first row, Lemma 5 states that the indicator of a rank-r unital space lies in RM(r−5,r−1). The CCZ support argument uses the corresponding stabilizer-only moment constraints, giving c∈RM(s−4,s), with n=|c|−c(0). Logical CCZ has an odd logical triple, so the original T-code classification does not itself provide the CCZ exclusion.
