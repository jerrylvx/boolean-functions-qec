\turn{a. The cube code (9 min)}
\cue{0--1.5 min / 1}{Qubits indexed by the points of $\mathbb F_2^3$}{Words are functions on the points. Steps: u1, u2, u3, then the constant word. They span RM(1,3).}
\cue{1.5--3 min / 2}{CSS codes from two nested binary codes}{Define the logical basis, the X and Z checks, and d\_X, d\_Z.}
\cue{3--4.5 min / 3}{The cube code $\llbracket 8,3,2\rrbracket$}{K has rows u1, u2, u3, which is why qubits are points. Z000 Z100 gives d\_Z = 2.}
\cue{4.5--5 min / 4}{Transversal gates}{Product of single-qubit gates. A fault stays on one qubit. Define T and CCZ.}
\cue{5--6 min / 5}{The signed weight gives the phase}{T sites minus T-dagger sites. The gate condition mod 8.}
\cue{6--7.5 min / 6}{Choosing the signs on the cube}{p is the logical word of label 111. Distance to p is 4 for every other affine word.}
\cue{7.5--8.5 min / 7}{Weights of sums expand into overlaps}{The bit identity, summed over positions. Terms with four or more words vanish mod 8.}
\cue{8.5--9 min / 8}{The gate condition becomes conditions on generator rows}{Mod 8, mod 4, mod 2. Define the bracket. The cube passes.}
\turn{b. History and the CH conditions (5 min)}
\cue{9--11 min / 9}{Historical context}{Cite the CSS-T work of Camps-Moreno, L\'opez, Matthews et al.}
\cue{11--12.5 min / 10}{Triorthogonal matrix and triorthogonal space}{Nezami-Haah puncture parent spaces. We fix S first and find K.}
\cue{12.5--14 min / 11}{Conditions for quasi-transversal CCZ}{Mod 2 reduction gives the nine parities. A physical layer needs the signed congruences.}

\turn{c. Results and finite geometry (8 min)}
\cue{14--15.5 min / 12}{Result: a 48-qubit code with a transversal T/T$^\dagger$ CCZ}{39 to 46 excluded by Wills, Jain and Singh.}
\cue{15.5--17 min / 13}{Fix S using finite/projective geometry code}{Four steps. Only the logical rows are searched.}
\cue{17--18 min / 14}{Projectivity rules out weight-one and weight-two Z logicals}{Parity-check argument. Covers d\_Z only.}
\cue{18--19.5 min / 15}{Complement of a projective subspace}{63 points, remove the 15 with w = 00, three blocks of 16.}
\cue{19.5--21 min / 16}{Hyperplane sections and codeword weights}{u1 gives 24, w1 gives 32. Both divisible by 8.}
\cue{21--22 min / 17}{The geometric construction produces a family}{p = 4, q = 2 gives 48. Lengths 48 to 496.}
\turn{d. Column picture and search (9.5 min)}
\cue{22--23.5 min / 18}{Column indicators from the generator matrix}{Show G for the cube. chi\_G = y because every x occurs with y = 1.}
\cue{23.5--25.5 min / 19}{The CH conditions are inner products with monomials}{Overlap parity is an inner product. Coset of RM(r-4, r).}
\cue{25.5--27.5 min / 20}{Logical rows in the column picture}{Define the logical label, T\_K, T\_S and the bracket. Step 2 shows K1.}
\cue{27.5--30 min / 21}{Finding the logical rows}{Algorithm 1: V\_P, pairs, then Gaussian elimination for kappa 3. Witness gives d\_Z = 3.}
\cue{30--31.5 min / 22}{Solving for the T/T$^\dagger$ pattern}{Binary unknowns, linear over Z4. 26 T plus 22 T-dagger.}
